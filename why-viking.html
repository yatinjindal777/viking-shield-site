Why Viking

Viking Shield is built on one idea: good security is proactive, and proactive security is a procedure, not a promise.

What you get

Always proactive
Guards patrol, check and report before something happens. Problems get found on rounds, not discovered in the morning.
Operations run on SOPs
Written standard operating procedures for every post: patrols, logs, shift handovers, incidents and escalation.
Custom site policies
Before a guard starts, your site gets its own written policy, like the sample beside this list.
A dedicated account manager
One named contact for your site, who knows your property, reviews the reports and answers when you call.
Trained guards
Trained and briefed on your site policy before their first shift, and held to it every shift after.
Competitive pricing
A clear rate for the coverage your site actually needs, with no padding and no surprise extras.

How a new site starts.

From the first call to the first shift, every step is written down.

  1. Call or book

    Tell dispatch about the site, the hours and how urgent it is.

  2. Site policy

    We agree access rules, checks and contacts, and write them into a site policy.

  3. Guard briefing

    Guards are briefed on the policy and the SOPs before they start.

  4. Reporting

    Your account manager follows every report and checks in on the post.
