The Viking standard

Proactive is a procedure.

Anyone can say "proactive". On a Viking Shield site it's written down, checked on every round and reported to your account manager.

  1. Operations run on SOPs

    Written procedures for patrols, logs, handovers and incidents. The standard doesn't change with the shift.

  2. Custom site policies

    Post orders written for your property: who gets in, what gets checked, who gets called.

  3. A dedicated account manager

    One person who knows your site, reads every report and answers your calls.

  4. Trained guards

    Briefed on your site policy before their first shift, and held to it after.

  5. Competitive pricing

    A clear rate for the coverage you need, with no padding and no surprise extras.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Still have a question? Call dispatch at (416) 000-0000 or email info@vikingshieldsecurity.com.

How quickly can you put a guard on site?

Book online or call dispatch. We call back to confirm guards, hours and rate, and we take same-night requests. If we can cover tonight, we tell you on that call.

Are Viking Shield guards licensed?

Yes. Every guard on a post holds a valid Ontario security guard licence, as Ontario law requires.

What is a site policy?

Written post orders for your property: who gets in, what gets checked and when, and who gets called. Guards are briefed on it before their first shift.

Will I get reports?

Yes. Every shift files a written report to your dedicated account manager, and incidents are reported the same night.

Can I book short-term coverage, or only contracts?

Both. Book a guard for tonight, for this week, or on an ongoing contract.

How is pricing set?

A clear rate based on the coverage your site needs: number of guards, hours and post type. No padding and no surprise extras.

Which areas do you cover?

Toronto and 140+ communities across Ontario, from Windsor to Ottawa and up to Thunder Bay. See the locations page for city pages and the full list.
