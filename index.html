The Viking standard

Proactive is a procedure.

Anyone can say "proactive". On a Viking Shield site it's written down, checked on every round and reported to your account manager.

  1. Operations run on SOPs

    Written procedures for patrols, logs, handovers and incidents. The standard doesn't change with the shift.

  2. Custom site policies

    Post orders written for your property: who gets in, what gets checked, who gets called.

  3. A dedicated account manager

    One person who knows your site, reads every report and answers your calls.

  4. Trained guards

    Briefed on your site policy before their first shift, and held to it after.

  5. Competitive pricing

    A clear rate for the coverage you need, with no padding and no surprise extras.
