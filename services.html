Concierge security

A concierge guard is often the first person a resident or visitor meets. We staff the desk with guards who look the part, know your building's rules and keep a clean record of who came and went.

  • Front-desk presence and a professional first impression
  • Visitor, contractor and delivery logs
  • Package and key control
  • Lobby, garage and amenity checks on a set schedule
  • Written shift reports to your account manager

Mobile patrol

Mobile patrol covers sites that don't need a guard all night but can't be left alone. Patrols follow a route and schedule built for your property, and every check is logged.

  • Scheduled and random patrols on a defined route
  • Lock-up and unlock checks
  • Alarm response and site verification
  • Parking enforcement where your policy allows it
  • Time-stamped incident and patrol reports

03 · Site security
03 / 04

Site security

Construction and vacant sites attract theft and trespass after hours. Site guards control the gate, check the perimeter and keep the site manager informed.

  • Gate and access control for workers and deliveries
  • Perimeter, fencing and equipment checks
  • Overnight and weekend watch on vacant or active sites
  • Fire-watch posts when required
  • Incident reports and photos for the site manager

Retail security

Retail security protects people, stock and the store's reputation. Our guards are trained to deter first and de-escalate when it counts.

  • Visible, uniformed presence on the floor and at entrances
  • Loss-prevention patrols and high-value area checks
  • Calm, policy-led de-escalation
  • Opening and closing escorts
  • Incident reports for store and district managers
