Do you provide security guards in Brantford?

How quickly can you put a guard on site in Brantford?

Book online or call dispatch. We call back to confirm guards, hours and rate, and we take same-night requests. If we can cover tonight, we tell you on that call.

Are Viking Shield guards licensed?

Yes. Every guard on a post holds a valid Ontario security guard licence, as Ontario law requires.

What is a site policy?

Written post orders for your property: who gets in, what gets checked and when, and who gets called. Guards are briefed on it before their first shift.

Yes. Every shift files a written report to your dedicated account manager, and incidents are reported the same night.

Both. Book a guard for tonight, for this week, or on an ongoing contract.

How is pricing set?

A clear rate based on the coverage your site needs: number of guards, hours and post type. No padding and no surprise extras.
